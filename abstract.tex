\begin{abstract}
We present a search for the $B\rightarrow X_{s} \nu \bar{\nu}$ decay, where $X_{s}$ is a hadronic system containing an s quark, using data collected with the Belle~II detector at the SuperKEKB accelerator.
The search uses a data sample corresponding to an integrated luminosity of $365~\textrm{fb}^{-1}$ collected at the $\Upsilon(4S)$ resonance.
An additional off-resonance sample, collected at a center-of-mass energy $60~\textrm{MeV}$ below the $\Upsilon(4S)$ resonance, is used to correct and validate the modeling of the $e^+e^-\to q\bar{q}$ continuum background.
We train a machine-learning classifier to separate signal from background and extract the signal yield from the two-dimensional distribution of the classifier output and the reconstructed mass of the $X_{s}$ system.
We find no significant signal and set upper limits on the partial branching fractions in three regions of the true $X_{s}$ mass, $M_{X_{s}}$.
For $0.0 < M_{X_{s}} < 0.6\gevcc$, $0.6 < M_{X_{s}} < 1.0\gevcc$, and $1.0\gevcc < M_{X_{s}}$, the upper limits at 90\% confidence level are $2.2 \times 10^{-5}$, $9.3 \times 10^{-5}$, and $30.9 \times 10^{-5}$, respectively.
Combining the three mass regions, we obtain an upper limit on the branching fraction of $B(B \to X_s \nu \bar{\nu}) < 3.3 \times 10^{-4}$ at 90\% confidence level.
\end{abstract}
